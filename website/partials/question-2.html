For a first-order gas phase decomposition reaction \( A(g) \to 2B(g) + C(g) \), the initial pressure is \( P_0 \) and total pressure at time \( t \) is \( P_t \). The rate constant \( k \) is:
k = \frac{2.303}{t} \log_{10}\left(\frac{2 P_0}{3 P_0 - P_t}\right)
A Half-life \( t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{k} \) is independent of initial partial pressure
B Half-life is directly proportional to initial concentration
C The unit of rate constant is \( \text{mol}\cdot\text{L}^{-1}\cdot\text{s}^{-1} \)
D The reaction rate remains constant as time progresses
