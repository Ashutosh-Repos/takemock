Derivation Analysis

Let \( I = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx \). Multiply by an equivalent integral over \( y \):

\( I^2 = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-y^2} dy = \iint_{\mathbb{R}^2} e^{-(x^2+y^2)} dx\,dy \).

Switching to polar coordinates \( (r, \theta) \) with area element \( r \, dr \, d\theta \): \( I^2 = \int_0^{2\pi} d\theta \int_0^\infty e^{-r^2} r \, dr = 2\pi \cdot \left[ -\frac{1}{2} e^{-r^2} \right]_0^\infty = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \pi \). Taking the positive square root gives \( I = \sqrt{\pi} \).
