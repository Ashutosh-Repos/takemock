Derivation Analysis

The effective radial potential for central force motion is $V_{\text{eff}}(r) = \frac{L^2}{2mr^2} - \frac{k}{r}$.

For total mechanical energy $E < 0$, turning points $\dot{r} = 0$ exist at $r_{\min}$ and $r_{\max}$. The trajectory is bounded between these radii, forming a closed ellipse with the central force at one focus (Kepler's first law). When $E = 0$, the orbit is parabolic; when $E > 0$, hyperbolic.
