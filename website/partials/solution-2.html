Derivation Analysis

The differential rate equation for a first-order reaction is $-\frac{d[A]}{dt} = k[A]$.

Integrating from $t=0$ to $t$ gives $[A]_t = [A]_0 e^{-kt}$. At half-life $t_{1/2}$, $[A]_t = \frac{1}{2}[A]_0$, yielding $t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{k} \approx \frac{0.693}{k}$. This is completely independent of initial concentration or pressure.
