Derivation Analysis

The differential rate equation for a first-order reaction is \( -\frac{d[A]}{dt} = k[A] \).

Integrating with limits from \( t=0 \) to \( t \) yields \( [A]_t = [A]_0 e^{-kt} \). At half-life \( t_{1/2} \), \( [A]_t = \frac{1}{2}[A]_0 \), giving \( t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 2}{k} \approx \frac{0.693}{k} \). This expression contains no concentration term, making half-life independent of initial concentration or initial partial pressure.
