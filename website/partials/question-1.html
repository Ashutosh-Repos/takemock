A particle of mass \( m \) moves in an attractive central force field \( V(r) = -\frac{k}{r} \). The effective radial potential and total orbital energy are:
E = \frac{1}{2} m \dot{r}^2 + \frac{L^2}{2 m r^2} - \frac{k}{r}
A \( E < 0 \) results in a bound elliptical Keplerian orbit
B \( E = 0 \) results in a circular stable orbit
C \( E > 0 \) gives a closed periodic trajectory
D Orbital angular momentum \( L \) is not conserved
